Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

BrandCraft AI ("we", "our", "the app") helps startups and small businesses build a brand identity. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a securely hashed password if you sign up with email.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Content you create: the business details you enter and the names, taglines and marketing content generated for you.

How we use your information

  • To create and sign you in to your account.
  • To generate and save your brand content.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

AI processing

When AI features are enabled, the text you enter to generate brand content may be sent to an AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result. Please do not enter sensitive personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to run the app (such as hosting and the AI provider), or if required by law.

Storage and security

Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no online service can be completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your account and data at any time by emailing us at the address below.

Children

BrandCraft AI is not intended for children under 13.
