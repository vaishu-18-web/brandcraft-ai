Privacy Policy

BrandCraft AI · Last updated: October 14, 2026

BrandCraft AI ("we", "our", "the app") is a branding tool for startups and small businesses, available at https://brandcraft-ai.onrender.com. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you create an account.
  • Password: if you sign up with email, your password is stored only as a secure hash. We cannot read it.
  • Google sign-in: if you use "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Tool inputs: the text you type into the tools (business type, keywords, taglines, marketing text, chat messages) so that we can generate results.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To generate brand names, logos, taglines, marketing content, sentiment analysis and chatbot replies.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In is used to log you in if you choose it.
  • AI providers: when AI mode is enabled, the text you enter in a tool may be sent to an AI provider (such as Anthropic) to produce a result. Please do not enter sensitive personal information.
  • Hosting: the app is hosted on Render.

Data storage and security

Account information is stored on our server. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. This is a demo project, so saved accounts may be reset when the server restarts.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing us at the address below.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy
